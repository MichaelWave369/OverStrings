MUDRA ATLAS

Rung 6 · Historical sources first · Gesture geometry second

Hands carry histories, not universal command codes.

This is an educational collection of documented gestures from Buddhist iconography and Indian classical dance, with representative physical descriptions, source links, and potential WebXR tracking constraints. Traditional meanings are culturally situated. They are not scientific proof of physiological effects.

Not a live detector: these references do not automatically recognize gestures, activate game actions, or claim Quest 3 validation. Hand tracking remains opt-in in the separate playground.

Future research, not invented certainty

The source index also records all 28 single-hand hastas enumerated in the Abhinaya Darpaṇa and the ten hatha-yoga mudra/bandha practices of the Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā. Those ten are often full-body practices, not ten finger poses. We will not add an automatic classifier until there are source-reviewed variants, multiple real Quest captures, calibrated thresholds and opt-in safety gates.
